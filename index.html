AI Portfolio Manager

Okane

A patient, long-term AI portfolio manager living inside your Obsidian vault. He reads your research, forms conviction slowly, and does nothing most of the time.

Workflow

How It Works

Okane runs as a Claude Code agent. You feed it fundamental research and Nick decides when to act. The loop is deliberately slow.

Research →
Read →
(Maybe) Act →
Record

Daily checks are banned. Nick moves on conviction or kill condition breach, not noise.

Getting Started

Quick Start

Three steps to go from zero to a deployed portfolio.

1

Add research to the Knowledge Base

Drop earnings transcripts, 10-K filings, and analyst notes into the knowledge_base/ folders. Plain text works. Copy-paste is fine. Longer is better than shorter.

2

Initialize Nick

Run the init command. Nick reads the entire Knowledge Base, forms investment theses, and deploys $10,000 across 3–10 stocks.

/nick-init ⎘
3

Maintain the cycle

Keep feeding research. Run the weekly check after each weekend. Run the quarterly review after each earnings release.

/nick-weekly ⎘ /nick-quarterly AAPL ⎘

Reference

Commands

Four commands drive everything. Click any card for details.

/nick-init ▾
Once, at the start
Reads entire Knowledge Base, forms theses, deploys $10,000 across 3–10 stocks.

Nick reads every document in full, generates an investment thesis with mandatory kill conditions for each stock, then allocates capital across his highest-conviction picks. No price data is used — only fundamental business quality.

/nick-weekly ▾
Every weekend
Scans new KB inputs, checks kill conditions, acts only if triggered.

Nick reviews any new research added since the last check. He cross-references it against the kill conditions on every held position. Action is taken only if a kill condition is breached or a massive new conviction emerges.

/nick-quarterly ▾
After each earnings release
Deep-reads new filings for a specific ticker, delivers thesis verdict.

Pass a ticker symbol to get a focused deep-dive. Nick reads the new 10-Q or 10-K and the latest earnings transcript, then evaluates whether the original thesis is intact, strengthened, or broken. Example: /nick-quarterly AAPL

/watchlist-manager ▾
Anytime
Add, remove, or update tickers on Nick’s monitoring list.

Manages the watch list — tickers Nick is monitoring but hasn’t invested in yet. Watch list entries progress through stages: watching → researching → ready → invested or passed.

Philosophy

Nick's Rules

Five principles that govern every decision Nick makes.

  1. Do nothing most of the time. Acts only on deep conviction or kill condition breach.
  2. Deep reading first. Reads every source document in full before forming a view.
  3. Kill conditions are mandatory. Every position has explicit exit criteria.
  4. Intellectual honesty. Says “I don’t know” rather than guessing.
  5. Blind to your real portfolio. Analyzes only what’s in the Knowledge Base.
