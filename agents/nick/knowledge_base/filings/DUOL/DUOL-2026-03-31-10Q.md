## Quarterly Results

CEO Luis von Ahn characterized Q1 2026 as solid execution on the strategy outlined the prior quarter: prioritizing teaching better and growing the audience, with speaking practice now a core part of the learning experience. The company frames 2026 as an "investment year" focused on AI-driven product improvements, expanded subjects, and user growth over near-term bookings growth and profit margins.

## Notable Changes

### Product & Strategic Updates

1. **Speaking Practice now core to the product:**
   - Video Call (for paid subscribers) continues to improve; average words spoken per user more than doubled YoY
   - Expanding Video Call access to Super subscribers
   - New features: Spoken Tokens (speak answers instead of tapping), Flashcards (spoken recall), Speaking Adventures (real-world speaking tasks)

2. **Content scaling via AI:**
   - Published 20,500 course units in Q1 2026 alone, up from 7,100/quarter in 2025 and 1,800/quarter in 2024
   - Content now extends to Duolingo Score 129 (CEFR B2, professional proficiency) across 9 most-learned languages
   - AI tools enabling simultaneous changes across many courses

3. **New subjects gaining traction:**
   - Chess reached ~7M DAUs in under a year
   - Duolingo Math positioned as parent-paid supplemental product
   - Music also available

4. **Mastery checking:**
   - Added end-of-unit mastery assessments that prevent progression until concepts are mastered
   - Provides data signal on content quality for iteration

5. **Share repurchase program:**
   - $400M authorization announced February 2026
   - Repurchased ~514,000 shares (~$50.6M) through May 1, 2026
   - Represents more than 100% of 2025 net dilution

### Key Concerns

- Gross margin guided to decline from 73.0% in Q1 to ~71.0% in Q2 and ~69.0% by Q4 2026 as AI feature use expands
- Q2 bookings growth guided to just 5.8% (tough comparable from Energy rollout, price increase, and strong ads in prior year)
- DET revenue declining (-6% YoY); IAPs declining (-11% YoY)
- Top-of-funnel (MAU) growth decelerated significantly to 6% YoY
- Stock-based compensation remains elevated (~15% of revenue expected for full-year 2026)
