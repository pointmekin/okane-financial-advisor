## Business Overview

Duolingo is a technology company and the leading mobile learning platform globally. Founded by Luis von Ahn and Severin Hacker, the company's flagship app has organically become the world's most popular way to learn languages and the top-grossing Education app on both Google Play and the Apple App Store.

**Key characteristics:**
- Mission: "Develop the best education in the world and make it universally available"
- 900+ employees including 430+ engineers as of December 31, 2025
- Freemium business model: free access to learning content, subscription for premium features
- 250+ total language courses offered to more than 130 million MAUs
- Expanding beyond languages into Math, Music, and Chess
- Nearly 2 billion exercises completed daily by learners
- According to HolonIQ, consumer spend on language learning is expected to reach ~$123 billion
- In the US, more people learn a language using Duolingo than the number of K-12 students taking a foreign language class

**Revenue model:**
- Subscription (Super and Max tiers) - primary revenue driver
- Advertising (display and video ads served to free users)
- Duolingo English Test (online English proficiency assessment)
- In-App Purchases (virtual goods)

**Dual flywheel strategy:**
1. Learning flywheel: Scale of learner base enables data-driven improvements to engagement and efficacy, driving organic growth
2. Investment flywheel: Word-of-mouth growth allows capital focus on product innovation rather than marketing

**Platform distribution:**
- Apple processed 61.6% of total revenues, Google 23.4%, Stripe 10.3%
- US revenue accounted for 38% of total; no other single country exceeded 10%

## Risk Factors (Top 5)

1. **User Growth & Engagement Risk:** If Duolingo fails to keep existing users or add new users, or if users decrease engagement or do not convert to paying users, revenue and financial results could be significantly harmed.

2. **Competitive Pressure:** The online language learning industry is highly competitive with low switching costs and a consistent stream of new products and entrants. Innovation by competitors may disrupt the business.

3. **AI Technology Risk:** Development, implementation, and use of AI/ML technologies (including third-party technologies) may not be successful, which could impair competitive positioning, result in reputational harm, and adversely affect the business. AI also introduces execution risk around rising infrastructure costs.

4. **Platform Dependency:** Duolingo relies primarily on Apple App Store and Google Play Store to distribute products and collect payments. Changes to terms, conditions, or pricing by these platforms could materially harm the business. Three providers (Apple, Google, Stripe) process ~95% of total revenue.

5. **Investment & Margin Pressure:** Costs continue to grow, and some investments reduce operating margin and profitability. If investments are not successful, business and financial performance could be harmed. Gross margin declined from 72.8% to 72.2% in FY 2025 due to increased AI costs.

## Competitive Position

**Strengths:**
- World's most popular language learning app with unmatched organic reach
- ~130M MAUs and 52.7M DAUs creating massive data and network effects
- Strong gamification driving industry-leading engagement (39.6% DAU/MAU ratio)
- Zero debt, $1.0B+ cash position
- Operating margin improving (from -2.5% to 13.1% over 3 years)
- Content creation increasingly AI-powered (10x course unit output in 2 years)
- Expanding beyond languages into Math, Music, and Chess
- Top-grossing Education app on both major app stores

**Weaknesses:**
- Heavy concentration on Apple/Google platform economics
- Declining gross margins as AI costs scale
- Single operating segment; limited revenue diversification beyond subscriptions
- Relatively high SBC dilution (~3.5-4% annually expected in 2026)
- US revenue concentration at 38%

**Peers for comparison:**
- Chegg (CHGG): Academic-cycle dependent, lacks Duolingo's high-frequency engagement
- Coursera (COUR): Institutional partnerships, more cyclical revenue mix
