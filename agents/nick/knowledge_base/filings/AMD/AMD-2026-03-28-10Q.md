## Revenue by Segment

| Segment | Q1 2026 Revenue | Q1 2025 Revenue | YoY Change |
|---------|----------------|----------------|------------|
| Data Center | $5.775B | $3.686B | +57% |
| Client and Gaming | $3.605B | $2.934B | +23% |
| Embedded | $873M | $823M | +6% |
| **Total** | **$10.253B** | **$7.441B** | **+38%** |

**Data Center (57% of revenue):** Driven by strong AMD Instinct GPU shipments and continued EPYC server CPU adoption. Hyperscaler demand for AI training and inference remains the primary growth driver.

**Client and Gaming (35% of revenue):** Growth from Ryzen desktop and mobile processor sales. Gaming-related revenue continued to decline as console cycle matures.

**Embedded (9% of revenue):** Modest recovery with 6% YoY growth, suggesting the inventory correction in industrial/embedded markets may be bottoming.

## Notable Changes

1. **Meta Partnership Announced:** AMD and Meta announced a multi-year partnership for up to 6GW of AMD Instinct GPU deployment, estimated at $60B. This represents one of the largest AI infrastructure deals in the industry and significantly expands AMD's hyperscaler customer base.

2. **MI450 Sampling to Customers:** AMD confirmed that the next-generation MI450 GPU is now sampling to customers. Customer forecasts for MI450 are exceeding AMD's initial expectations, suggesting strong demand for the product launching in H2 2026.

3. **Data Center GPU Momentum:** Data Center segment grew 57% YoY to $5.775B, making it AMD's largest segment (56% of total revenue). The segment is now on an annualized run rate of over $23B.

4. **Embedded Segment Recovery:** The 6% YoY growth in Embedded is the first positive growth in several quarters, indicating the cyclical downturn may be ending.

5. **Gross Margin Expansion:** GAAP gross margin expanded 3 percentage points YoY to 53%, driven by favorable product mix shift toward higher-margin Data Center products.

6. **Balance Sheet Strengthening:** Cash position increased to $12.3B from $10.6B at year-end, while total debt slightly declined to $3.2B. Net cash position now approximately $9.1B.

### Key Forward-Looking Points

- **Data Center:** Expected to remain the primary growth driver with accelerating Instinct GPU deployments. MI450 launch in H2 2026 is a major catalyst.
- **Client and Gaming:** Expected to remain stable with potential upside from new Ryzen product launches.
- **Embedded:** Expected to continue gradual recovery as inventory normalization completes.
- **Full Year 2026:** AMD expects continued strong growth driven by AI infrastructure demand, with Data Center revenue potentially exceeding $20B for the full year.
- **Competitive Positioning:** AMD views the OpenAI and Meta partnerships as validation of its competitive position in AI accelerators against NVIDIA.
