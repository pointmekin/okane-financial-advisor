## Notable Changes

1. **Blackwell Ramp**: Blackwell B200/B300 products began volume shipments, contributing significantly to Data Center revenue growth.

2. **Manufacturing Commitments**: Total manufacturing commitments reached $50.3B, reflecting long-term supply agreements with TSMC and packaging partners for advanced nodes.

3. **Cloud Service Commitments**: $26B in cloud service commitments, indicating hyperscaler demand visibility extends multiple quarters.

4. **Strategic Investments**:
   - $6.5B in investment commitments
   - $5B planned investment in Intel (subject to regulatory approval)
   - Letter of intent to invest in OpenAI
   - Agreement to invest up to $10B in Anthropic

5. **Export Control Impact**: H20-related charges and revenue restrictions continued to affect the China market, though the full $4.5B charge was recognized in Q4 FY2026 (subsequent to this filing).

6. **Groq Integration**: The $17B Groq license agreement began integration, with $14.4B allocated to goodwill on the balance sheet.

## Guidance/Outlook

As of the Q3 FY2026 filing (October 2025), NVIDIA provided the following forward-looking indicators:

- **Q4 FY2026 Revenue**: Expected to be approximately $68B (actual result: $68.1B, +73% YoY)
- **Data Center Demand**: Continued to exceed supply with long lead times for Blackwell products
- **Q1 FY2027 Outlook**: Approximately $78B revenue (subsequently guided on Feb 2026 earnings call)
- **Rubin Platform**: Announced for H2 FY2027 as the next-generation architecture beyond Blackwell Ultra
- **Gross Margin**: Expected to remain in the low-to-mid 70s range, with pressure from product mix transitions

**Post-filing developments:**
- Q4 FY2026 actual revenue: $68.1B (beat guidance)
- Q1 FY2027 guidance raised to ~$78B
- US government expanded export controls on H20 and H200 products
